AI Investing Lab

A private investing research and paper-trading lab with human review.

What the application does

AI Investing Lab organizes research, tracks virtual portfolios, records decisions, and reconciles paper-trading activity. It uses Google Sheets as its operational record and Google Drive for research files and reports. Proposed paper trades require human approval and execution checks.

Google account access

The owner authorizes the application to read and update its control spreadsheet and to read and create research artifacts in designated Google Drive folders. The application is intended for the owner's personal use; this website does not provide a public trading service or accept account registrations.
