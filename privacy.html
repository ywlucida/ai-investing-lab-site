AI Investing Lab

Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026.

AI Investing Lab is a personal research and paper-trading application operated by the Google account owner. This policy describes the application's use of Google user data.

Data accessed and purpose

With the owner's consent, the application uses Google Sheets and Google Drive APIs to read and update its control spreadsheet, access designated research folders, and create research files and reports. Records include securities, market snapshots, research events, virtual portfolio ledgers, configuration, run logs, trade proposals, approvals, and reconciliation results. OAuth credentials allow the authorized runtime to maintain this connection.

The current authorization requests Sheets and Drive scopes that can permit broader account access than the designated files. Application configuration limits normal operations to the selected spreadsheet and folders. These permissions are used to provide the application's bookkeeping, research storage, audit history, and reporting functions.

Storage and service providers

Operational records and research artifacts are stored in the owner's Google Sheets and Drive. Runtime credentials are stored privately on the owner's computer or as GitHub Actions secrets for hosted operation. Authorized GitHub Actions jobs process records to operate the application. Selected proposal details are included in private GitHub review issues and email notifications to the owner through Gmail. Alpaca provides paper-trading account and market-data services; Google OAuth credentials are not sent to Alpaca.

Sharing and permitted use

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. Data is used for the application's described functions and disclosed to the owner and service providers as needed to perform those functions. The public website contains only application information and policies, not private account records or credentials.

AI Investing Lab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Security

API connections use HTTPS. Credentials are kept separate from public website files and source code, and hosted credentials are stored as repository secrets. Access depends on the owner's Google and GitHub account permissions. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Retention and deletion

Research and audit records are retained until the owner removes them. The owner can stop the runtime, delete stored records from Google Drive, Sheets, GitHub, and local storage, and remove runtime credentials. Provider backups and retention policies may continue to apply. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase previously stored records.

Your controls and contact

The owner can revoke Google authorization in Google Account connections. For questions or assistance with deletion, contact ywlucida@gmail.com.

Changes

This page will be updated when the application's data practices change. The effective date identifies the latest revision.
